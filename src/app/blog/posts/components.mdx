### Method 3: Browser Extensions

Several browser extensions add download buttons directly to YouTube pages. Search your browser's extension store for "YouTube downloader" — just read the reviews first, some are sketchier than others.

## Legal Considerations

Here's the honest answer: downloading YouTube videos for personal offline use falls in a gray area. I'm not a lawyer, but the general rule I follow is: only download content you own or have clear permission to use. If it's your own video, a royalty-free track, or something in the public domain, you're fine. If it's a copyrighted music video you're planning to redistribute, don't.
