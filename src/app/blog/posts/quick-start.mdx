## Study Tips

A few things I wish someone had told me:

1. Use DEG mode for geometry problems, RAD mode for calculus
2. Double-check parentheses for complex expressions — one missing bracket throws everything off
3. Use the history to review your work before submitting
4. Practice mental math for simple operations — it'll save you time on exams
