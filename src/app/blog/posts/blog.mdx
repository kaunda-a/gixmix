## Why Pay for Photoshop?

Adobe Photoshop costs $22/month. For freelancers, students, or casual users, that adds up fast. Luckily, free alternatives have matured to the point where they can handle most professional workflows.

## The 10 Best Free Alternatives

### 1. GIMP
The gold standard of free image editing. Layers, masks, brushes, color correction — GIMP does it all. Available on Windows, Mac, and Linux.

### 2. Photopea
Runs entirely in your browser. Supports PSD files natively. No installation required. Perfect for quick edits on any computer.

### 3. Krita
Built for digital painting but excellent for photo editing too. Industry-standard brush engine.

### 4. Paint.NET
Simple, intuitive, and powerful. Great for Windows users who find GIMP overwhelming.

### 5. Canva (Free Tier)
Browser-based design tool. Thousands of templates. Perfect for social media graphics.

### 6. Pixlr
Another browser-based editor. Comes in two versions: Pixlr X (beginner) and Pixlr E (advanced).

### 7. Inkscape
Vector graphics editor. The free alternative to Adobe Illustrator. Works great alongside raster editors.

### 8. Darktable
RAW image processing. Think Lightroom but free. Non-destructive editing workflow.

### 9. RawTherapee
Another RAW processor with powerful algorithms. Great for photographers.

### 10. PhotoScape X
All-in-one photo editor with batch processing, GIF creator, and screen capture tools.
