## The Problem with Human Passwords

90% of passwords can be cracked in under 6 hours. "Password123" and "qwerty" are still the most common passwords worldwide.

## What Makes a Password Strong?

- **Length**: 16+ characters (longer is always better)
- **Complexity**: Uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols
- **Randomness**: No dictionary words, no personal info
- **Uniqueness**: Never reuse passwords across sites
