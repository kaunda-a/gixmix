## The Problem with Human Passwords

I used to be terrible at passwords. My "system" was taking a favorite word, capitalizing the first letter, and adding a number at the end. You know, like "Password1" — that level of creativity. Then I checked haveibeenpwned.com and found my email in six different data breaches.

Turns out, 90% of passwords can be cracked in under 6 hours. "Password123" and "qwerty" are still the most common passwords worldwide. We're all doing it wrong.

## What Makes a Password Strong?

It's simpler than you think:

- **Length**: 16+ characters. Longer is always better than more complex.
- **Complexity**: Uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols — mix them up.
- **Randomness**: No dictionary words, no personal info (birthdays, pet names, etc.)
- **Uniqueness**: Never reuse passwords across sites. This is the big one.
