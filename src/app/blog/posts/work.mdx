## What Is Content Locking?

Content locking is a monetization method where premium content (downloads, advanced features, full guides) is "locked" behind a simple user action. The user completes a quick offer — like filling out a survey or submitting their email — and the content unlocks.

You earn money for each completed offer.

## Best Practices

- **Don't lock everything**: Let users use the basic tool for free, gate premium features
- **Test placement**: Try lockers at different points in the user flow
- **Mobile optimization**: Most traffic is mobile
- **Clear value prop**: Tell users exactly what they'll unlock

## Traffic Sources That Convert Best

1. **SEO traffic** — Highest intent, best conversion
2. **TikTok** — High volume, medium conversion
3. **YouTube tutorials** — Medium volume, high trust
4. **Reddit** — Niche traffic, good for specific tools
