**Pro tips for higher scan rates**:

- **Custom colors**: Match your brand colors — QR codes don't have to be black and white
- **Logo overlay**: Place your logo in the center for brand recognition
- **Call to action**: Add "Scan me" text near the code
- **Size matters**: Minimum 2cm x 2cm for print, larger for posters
- **Test always**: Print and test before mass production
