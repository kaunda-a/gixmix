## The Opportunity

I stumbled into the tools website world by accident. I needed a calculator that did one specific thing, couldn't find one that wasn't plastered with spammy ads, so I built my own. Then I thought, "huh, what if I built a few more?"

Turns out, free online tools websites generate millions of visits every single month. People search for "free calculator," "QR code generator," "password generator" — these aren't niche queries. They get searched hundreds of thousands of times a day. And nobody wants to pay for them.

The real trick — and the thing most people get wrong — is monetizing that traffic without ruining the experience. Slap too many ads on a tool page and people leave in three seconds. But do it right, and you've got a sustainable business.

## Monetization Methods

### 1. CPA Content Lockers (Highest Earnings)

This is what powers GixMix. A content locker sits behind your premium features — surveys, email submits, app installs — and users complete a quick action to unlock them. You get paid per completion.

**Here's the flow in practice**:
- Someone lands on your tool page from Google
- They use the basic version for free (this is important — never lock the core functionality)
- To unlock premium features or downloads, they complete a quick offer
- You earn anywhere from $0.50 to $25.00 per completion, depending on the offer and the user's country

**The tactics that made the biggest difference for me**:

- **Geo-targeting**: Route users from US/UK/AU to high-paying offers ($5-25), others to lower-tier offers ($0.50-2). Same effort, wildly different payouts.
- **Offer splitting**: Show 2 offers instead of 1. Sounds counterintuitive, but completion rates go up when people have a choice.
- **Retargeting**: Use pixel tracking to re-engage users who didn't complete. You'd be surprised how many come back.
- **A/B testing**: Test different locker positions, colors, and copy. A simple wording change doubled my conversion rate on one tool.

### 2. Display Ads

Networks like Google AdSense, Ezoic, or Mediavine. Lower earnings per visitor, but completely passive once set up. I use these as a baseline and supplement with lockers.

### 3. Affiliate Links

Recommend hosting, software, or services related to your tools. If you've got a tool that helps people build websites, link to hosting providers. Relevant recommendations convert way better than random banner ads.
