## The Opportunity

Free online tools websites generate millions of visits monthly. The reason? People always need quick utilities — calculators, converters, generators. And they don't want to pay for them.

The trick is monetizing that traffic without ruining the user experience.

## Monetization Methods

### 1. CPA Content Lockers (Highest Earnings)

This is what powers GixMix. A content locker gates premium features behind a quick user action (survey, email submit, app install).

**How it works**:
- User visits your tool page
- They use the basic version for free
- To unlock premium features or downloads, they complete an offer
- You earn $0.50 - $25.00 per completion

<ContentLocker title="Unlock Advanced Monetization Strategies" description="Complete a quick offer to access our premium guide">
**Advanced tactics that 10x your earnings**:

- **Geo-targeting**: Route users from US/UK/AU to high-paying offers ($5-25), others to lower-tier offers ($0.50-2)
- **Offer splitting**: Show 2 offers instead of 1 to increase completion rates
- **Retargeting**: Use pixel tracking to re-engage users who didn't complete
- **A/B testing**: Test different locker positions, colors, and copy
